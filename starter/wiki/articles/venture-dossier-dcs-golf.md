# Venture Dossier: DCS Golf

> **Bottom line:** There is a real, proven price band for hand-made premium bags: **USD 850 for MacKenzie's waxed-canvas Walker, USD 1,199.99 for Sun Mountain's full-grain leather bag, and EUR 1,515.95 for MacKenzie's full-leather Walker** (brand stores, re-checked 18 Nov 2026). In Dubai, a *synthetic* Vessel stand bag already retails at AED 2,195–2,295. At Nov 2026 Italian leather prices a **full-leather bag earns only ~14% DTC** and loses money through a retailer. The **waxed-canvas bag with leather trim earns ~29%** and is the hero product. Licensing (updated 13 Nov 2026): the AED 1,070 eTrader licence is officially for UAE/GCC nationals. Press reports say expats may hold it **for services only**, so selling bags needs a free-zone or mainland licence (Meydan FZ packages from AED 12,500). Prove demand first with corporate pre-orders. A licence is only worth buying once a deposit is in hand. New 18 Nov: priced like MacKenzie's leather bag (≈ AED 5,500), full leather earns ~31% DTC, so it works as a premium tier; and an Indian workshop's leather goods enter the UAE at **0% duty** under CEPA.

## Context
Decision informed: whether DCS Golf moves past Validate, at what price point and with what production model. Venture: hand-made leather and canvas golf bags and accessories, luxury positioning, home start in the UAE.

## Production inputs
- **Leather (re-checked 13 Nov 2026):** Italian veg-tanned Badalassi Carlo vacchetta is USD 229.99 per full hide. The reseller now lists the hide as 10–14+ sq ft, which works out to **≈ USD 16–23/sq ft** [17]. The 8 Nov figure of ~USD 15 assumed a larger hide, so it was too low. Imported (non-Italian) veg-tan sides cost USD 170 per 22–27 sq ft side, **≈ USD 6–8/sq ft** [18]. That is less than half the price, but the leather is less prestigious. Our calculation from listed prices; retail, before shipping and duty.
- **Waxed canvas (new):** Halley Stevensons Everwax 10oz costs CAD 19.95 per linear foot at 60" width [19]. That is ≈ CAD 65 per metre, or roughly **AED 170–180/m**. Our conversion is approximate and unverified. An 18oz Halley Stevensons luggage twill lists at USD 27.29, but the page does not say per yard or per metre [T3].
- **UAE leather sources (new):** Two local options may avoid the 5% duty and give a "made in the Emirates" story.
  - **Leather Studio (Salem Tannery), Al Hamra Industrial Zone, Ras Al Khaimah.** An 8-person finishing tannery that does custom colours, embossing and finishes on cow, goat and sheep crust. It has no minimum order, but prices are higher under 500 sq ft [20].
  - **Al Khaznah Leathers.** A UAE tannery making metal-free leather, with clients in fashion and leather goods (Khaleej Times sponsored supplement, May 2025) [21]. Whether it sells small lots is not stated.
  - Prices for both: **not published — request quotes**. GCC-origin goods are duty-free [10].
- **Customs:** 5% of CIF value; GCC-origin goods duty-free [PwC, T2].
- **CEPA origins (new, 18 Nov 2026):**
  - **India:** India's Council for Leather Exports reports that under the India–UAE CEPA (in force 1 May 2022) the UAE gives **0% duty** on Indian leather (HS 41) and leather products (HS 42) [34]. Bags (HS 4202) fall in Chapter 42, but the circular does not name them; check the HS line in the UAE schedule. The goods need an Indian certificate of origin and must meet CEPA rules of origin.
  - **Türkiye:** the UAE–Türkiye CEPA came into force around 31 Aug 2023 and removes or cuts duty on 82% of tariff lines [36]. Abu Dhabi Customs says Turkish-origin goods shipped directly are exempt per the schedule, claimed with a certificate or approved-exporter origin declaration (code 59 on the declaration) [35]. The rate for leather bags was not checked — use the Ministry's CEPA market-access dashboard.
  - Judgement: for a **finished bag made by an Indian or Turkish workshop**, CEPA removes the AED ~100–180 per bag of duty in the worked example; that is a small but real reason to favour those origins over China or Portugal (EU, no CEPA in force with the UAE as far as we know — unverified). **VAT** 5%, mandatory registration above AED 375,000 taxable supplies (T3 — confirm with FTA).
- **MOQ proxy:** Hudson Sutler (made overseas) needs 12 pieces for custom logo, 72 for custom made-to-order [T1] — a guide to what OEMs ask.
- **Labour hours per bag:** still not found from a credible source. MacKenzie built its bags by hand with 10–15 staff in Portland (c. 2016) [22]. Steurer & Jacoby say one bag takes "a few days to two weeks" to make, but that is lead time, not labour hours (2018) [23]. Time the first prototype.

## Worked example — unit economics, two bag designs (illustrative AED; re-priced 13 Nov 2026)
Assumptions (all judgement, to replace with real quotes):
- Italian leather at the USD 20/sq ft midpoint of the re-checked range, plus 15% waste.
- Labour at AED 50/hr: 20 hours for a full-leather bag, 14 hours for canvas with trim.
- Waxed canvas at AED 175/m.
- 5% duty on imported materials.
- Retail prices include 5% VAT. USD/AED 3.6725 (pegged).

| Line | A. Full leather, retail AED 4,400 | B. Waxed canvas + leather trim, retail AED 3,100 |
|---|---|---|
| Leather (A: 28.75 sq ft; B: 6.9 sq ft) × USD 20 × 3.6725 | 2,112 | 507 |
| Waxed canvas (B: 2.5 m) | — | 438 |
| Duty 5% on imported materials | 106 | 47 |
| Hardware, lining | 300 | 300 |
| Labour | 1,000 | 700 |
| Packaging, dust bag, card | 100 | 100 |
| **Unit cost** | **≈ 3,618** | **≈ 2,092** |
| Net price (retail ÷ 1.05) | 4,190 | 2,952 |
| **Gross margin DTC** | **≈ 572 (14%)** | **≈ 860 (29%)** |
| Through a retailer taking 45% | loss ≈ –1,313 | loss ≈ –468 |

How sensitive this is:
- Imported non-Italian veg-tan (~USD 7/sq ft) lifts design A's margin to about 39%. That swap trades away the "Italian leather" story.
- Every 5 hours of labour saved is worth AED 250 a bag.
- On the 8 Nov estimate (USD 15/sq ft), design A earned 27%. The price re-check halved it.
- **Price A like MacKenzie's leather bag (new, 18 Nov 2026).** MacKenzie sells its full-leather Walker at EUR 1,515.95 [30]. At an assumed ~4.3 AED per EUR (not verified this run) that is ≈ AED 6,500. Pricing design A at **AED 5,500** (still below MacKenzie): net 5,238 − cost 3,618 = **AED 1,620 a bag (31%)**. At AED 6,500: net 6,190, margin AED 2,572 (42%). Judgement: full leather is viable as a premium tier priced near MacKenzie, not at the AED 4,400 Sun Mountain level.

Implication: full leather only works as a **premium tier or bespoke corporate piece at AED 5,500+** (MacKenzie shows the market pays ≈ EUR 1,500 for it). The **canvas-with-trim bag is the hero**, sold DTC and to corporates. Accessories (headcovers, scorecard holders, valuables pouches) use offcuts and carry high margins, so they suit retail and gifting. No design works through a 45%-margin retailer at these prices without outsourced production.

Whether leather-goods retail is on DET's permitted-activity list was not checked |

- An eTrader cannot open a shop or sponsor visas [11]. Khaleej Times (2022) reported three visas only where ownership is 100% Emirati [26].
- Home production needs the landlord's written consent. Dubai Law 26/2007 lets a landlord evict for use other than the purpose let (Art. 25) [27]. Judgement: making leather goods at home (tools, dyes, noise) is a clear risk under a residential lease.
- **Our view:** the cheapest legal set-up for an expat is a free-zone trading licence for leather goods. Pair it with **contract production by a licensed maker** (a UAE workshop or an overseas OEM) rather than home manufacturing. Buy the licence only after the first corporate deposit; validation (prototypes, quotes, conversations) needs no licence.
- Market size (estimates only): global golf bags ≈ USD 1.25bn (2025), leather segment growing ~8%/yr; UAE ≈ USD 17–18m (derived from GM Insights shares). A second report (KBV) is ~2× larger — treat both as indicative.

## Riskiest assumptions (judgement) — test in this order
1. **Legal set-up:** confirm in writing that an expat eTrader cannot sell goods, and which free-zone activity covers own-brand leather goods made by a contractor. *Test:* DET enquiry via Invest in Dubai; Meydan FZ or another free zone quote; adviser.
2. **Price acceptance at AED 3,000–4,400** for a new brand. *Test:* 3 prototypes, 15 pre-orders with 50% deposit, or one corporate order of 10+.
3. **Make vs buy:** UAE hand-making vs an India/Turkey/Portugal workshop. *Test:* time one build; get 3 OEM quotes with MOQs.
4. **Climate durability** of leather and wax in UAE heat/sun. *Test:* leave prototypes in a car/golf cart for a summer month.
5. **Retail access** on acceptable terms. *Test:* 3 pro-shop conversations.

## Key takeaways
- Proven band: USD 850 (canvas) to ≈ EUR 1,500 (full leather) for hand-made bags; Dubai already pays AED 2,200+ for synthetic premium bags.
- At Nov 2026 Italian leather prices, a full-leather bag costs ≈ AED 3,600: ~14% DTC at AED 4,400, ~31% at AED 5,500. Canvas + trim earns ~29% at AED 3,100.
- Canvas-with-leather-trim hero + leather accessories is the viable range; UAE leather (RAK finisher) is worth quoting.
- eTrader/Intelaq do not fit an expat selling goods; budget ≈ AED 12,500+ for a free-zone licence, after demand is proven.
- Corporate gifting is the fastest, cheapest proof of demand.

## What this means for the empire
- Stay in **Validate**. Cap spend at prototype materials, then a free-zone licence (≈ AED 12,500+) once a corporate deposit exists. Judgement: total under AED 20k before revenue.
- Ask Leather Studio (RAK) for a quote on 50–100 sq ft. If it is close to imported Italian prices, the "finished in the UAE" story and zero duty favour it.
- Design the range around canvas + leather trim, made to order, sold DTC and to corporates; add retail only with ≥ 55% gross margin.
- Add a **full-leather premium tier at AED 5,500+**, made to order with a deposit; never stock it.
- When getting OEM quotes, ask **Indian (and Turkish) workshops first**: CEPA can make the finished bag duty-free. Ask each for a certificate of origin.
- Use Wednesday Circuit events as the launch showroom.
- Keep the books from day one ([[Double-Entry Accounting]]); revenue will sit far below the AED 375k VAT and CT thresholds initially ([[UAE Corporate Tax Essentials]]).
