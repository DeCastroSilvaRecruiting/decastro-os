# Property Investment Metrics

> **Bottom line:** A property is a small business. Measure it like one: what it earns after costs (net yield), what it returns on the cash *you* put in (cash-on-cash), whether rent comfortably covers the mortgage (DSCR), and what it returns over the whole hold including the exit (IRR).

## The metrics
| Metric | Formula | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Gross yield | Annual rent ÷ price | Headline; ignores costs |
| Net yield | (Rent × occupancy − annual costs) ÷ price | What the asset actually earns |
| NOI | Rent × occupancy − operating costs | Income before financing |
| Cash-on-cash | (NOI − annual debt service) ÷ cash invested | Return on *your* money |
| DSCR | NOI ÷ annual debt service | Can rent carry the loan? |
| LTV | Loan ÷ value | Leverage and refinancing risk |
| IRR | Rate that sets NPV of all flows to zero | Whole-hold return incl. exit |

## Worked example (illustrative AED)
Price 1,500,000 · 75% LTV · rent 110,000/yr · 5% vacancy · service charge 18,000 · other costs 6,000 · 4.5% over 25 years.
- Loan 1,125,000 → payment ≈ 6,253/month ≈ 75,000/yr
- NOI = 104,500 − 24,000 = **80,500** → net yield **5.4%** (gross 7.3%)
- Cash flow ≈ 80,500 − 75,000 = **5,500/yr**
- DSCR ≈ **1.07x** — thin; a rate rise or a vacant quarter tips it negative.

## Acquisition costs
Transfer fees, agency fees, mortgage registration and valuation fees add materially to the cash required, and are lost on day one. Current Dubai fee levels should be confirmed with the Dubai Land Department — a research item for Claude.

## Key takeaways
- Gross yield flatters; always compute net.
- Below ~1.2x DSCR the property is fragile.
- Leverage raises cash-on-cash *and* risk; stress-test rates and vacancy.
- IRR captures the exit; don't rely on growth you cannot justify.

## What this means for the empire
Only buy when net numbers work at today's rent and a higher rate. Treat expected price growth as upside, not as the case for the deal. Track every property in the portfolio with the same metrics so they can be compared and pruned.
