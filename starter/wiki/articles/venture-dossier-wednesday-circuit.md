# Venture Dossier: Wednesday Circuit

> **Bottom line:** The market backdrop is favourable — the UAE has 26 golf courses (13 in Dubai) running "near capacity" in peak season, and Dubai's business base is expanding fast (292,486 active Chamber members at end-2025, +13%). But that same scarcity is the core risk: a recurring Wednesday block at a premier course in Oct–Apr may not be available, and established, sponsor-funded formats (Emirates NBD CEO Masters, FORE Business, World Corporate Golf Challenge) already serve executives. **First test: written tee-block quotes from 3–4 clubs and 10 paid founding-member commitments.**

## Context
Decision informed: whether to move Wednesday Circuit from Validate to Plan, and what to test first. Venture: C-suite golf networking and corporate golf events in Dubai/UAE, later regional. See [[Venture Validation Framework]].

## Market and capacity (as of Nov 2026)
- **Courses:** 26 in the UAE — 13 in Dubai, 9 in Abu Dhabi (all courses, not only 18-hole championship courses); new capacity coming at Discovery Dunes, an Emaar course in Dubai South and a third 18-hole course at Jumeirah Golf Estates [1].
- **Utilisation:** Dubai Golf's CEO says courses run "at near capacity, especially during peak season" [1]. Emirates Golf Club reports **all membership categories full**; its Corporate Single membership includes 25 guest green-fee vouchers [3].
- **Golfers:** "22,000-plus resident golfers" (unsourced estimate) [10]. No current official rounds-per-season data found; the last credible study (Deloitte, 2018) counted ~410,000 rounds/yr across 9 Dubai clubs — stale.
- **Business base:** Dubai Chamber active members 292,486 at end-2025 (+13.2%) [7]; Dubai won 504 business-event bids in 2025 (+15%) and hosted 481 events [6].
- **Season:** usable peak Oct–Apr (~7 months); summer rates roughly half of peak [12].

## Competitors

| Club corporate memberships / golf days | Direct from clubs | Clubs can sell direct to corporates |
Judgement: the gap is not "golf for executives" but a **curated, recurring, invitation-only weekly format** with a controlled member mix (seniority, sector) — the CEO Masters is annual, FORE is SME-heavy.

## Licensing (to confirm with DET / adviser)
- **DET event permit** covers corporate and sport events, applied via DET ePermits [5]; fees and lead times not loaded from the official page.
- Commercial guide (T3) [13]: mainland DET licence (event organising) needed for mainland venues; venue NOC first; permit ~3–6 working days, apply ≥15 days before promotion; mainland licence ~AED 12–20k. Event management is **not** a free zone qualifying activity, so expect 9% CT above AED 375k — see [[UAE Corporate Tax Essentials]].
- Open: whether an invite-only members' day at a licensed club needs a permit each week.

## Worked example — one event (illustrative AED, assumptions labelled)
24 executives, premier course, peak season. Assumptions: green fee 1,000/head (mid of T3 ranges), lunch 80 + dinner 185 [2], photographer and pin flags 3,675 per event [2], price charged **2,500/head + VAT** (assumption to test).
- Cost per head: 1,000 + 265 + 153 = **≈ 1,418**
- Contribution per head ≈ 1,082 → **≈ AED 26,000 per event**
- 20 events per season → **≈ AED 520,000** contribution before staff, marketing and licence.
- Sensitivity: at 1,800/head contribution falls to ~9,200/event; one title sponsor at AED 100k/season covers ~4 events' margin. Sponsorship likely matters as much as fees.

## Riskiest assumptions (judgement) — test in this order
1. **Tee access:** a recurring peak-season block is bookable at premier courses at a known price. *Test:* written quotes from Emirates GC, Dubai Creek, JGE, Dubai Hills.
2. **Willingness to pay** a premium over a club golf day and over free sponsor events. *Test:* 10 founding members paying a deposit.
3. **Weekday availability** of C-suite for ~5 hours recurring. *Test:* pilot 3 consecutive Wednesdays, measure attendance.
4. **Sponsor appetite** (banks, wealth managers, auto). *Test:* 5 sponsor conversations with a one-page deck.
5. **Summer gap:** simulator/indoor formats (e.g. Topgolf, Five Iron) — untested.

## Key takeaways
- Scarce peak tee times are both the moat and the main risk.
- Incumbents are annual or SME-focused; a curated weekly C-suite format is the gap.
- Unit economics work at ~AED 2,500/head plus sponsorship; below ~AED 1,800 they get thin.
- Mainland DET licence + per-event permits likely; 9% CT applies above AED 375k.
- Only ~7 good months a year.

## What this means for the empire
- Stay in **Validate**. Spend under AED 20k: club quotes, a pilot of 3 events with a hand-picked list, founding-member deposits.
- Cross-sell with [[Venture Dossier: DCS Golf]] — event gifts and a captive luxury golf audience.
- Use a mainland operating company under the holdco described in [[UAE Holding Structures]].
