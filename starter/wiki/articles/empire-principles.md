# Empire Principles

> **Bottom line:** Wealth and trust are built by avoiding ruin, compounding small edges for a long time, and keeping promises. These principles are the filter every decision passes through. Claude may add or refine at most two per week, and only with evidence.

## Capital
1. **Avoid ruin before seeking return.** No upside justifies a meaningful chance of total loss. See [[Kelly Criterion and Position Sizing]].
2. **Fund the reserve before taking risk.** Operating cash and a reserve come first; only capital above that is deployable. See [[Treasury Liquidity Tiers]].
3. **Buy with a margin of safety.** Pay a price that still works if your estimates are wrong — they will be.
4. **Let compounding work uninterrupted.** The biggest cost is breaking the chain. See [[Compounding and the Rule of 72]].

## Ventures
5. **Test the riskiest assumption first, cheaply.** Do not build what you have not sold. See [[Venture Validation Framework]].
6. **Unit economics before scale.** A business that loses money per customer loses more with more customers.
7. **Prefer asymmetric bets.** Capped downside, open upside, many small experiments.

## Property
8. **Buy on net numbers.** Net yield, cash-on-cash and debt cover — not headline price growth. See [[Property Investment Metrics]].

## Governance & trust
9. **If it is not in the ledger, it did not happen.** Clean books for every entity. See [[Double-Entry Accounting]].
10. **Never miss a deadline you could have calendared.** Licences, filings and notice periods are trust made visible.
11. **Write decisions down before the outcome is known.** Measure your judgement honestly.

## Knowledge
12. **Primary sources over opinions.** Date every number. Revisit what you think you know.
