# UAE Corporate Tax Essentials

> **Bottom line:** Small ventures will usually pay little or no Corporate Tax: 0% on the first AED 375,000 of taxable profit, 9% above, and Small Business Relief (treat as zero taxable income if revenue ≤ AED 3m) now extends to tax periods ending on or before **31 Dec 2029** (MD 131/2026, announced 7 Aug 2026). But every taxable entity must still **register within 3 months of incorporation, file a return within 9 months of year end and keep records for 7 years** — the penalties for missing paperwork (AED 10,000 each for late registration and poor records) dwarf the tax.

## Context
Decision informed: how to set up and run DCS Golf, Wednesday Circuit and the Al Ain Ostrich Farm — as personal (sole) businesses or companies, mainland or free zone — and what compliance calendar to keep. Pairs with [[UAE Holding Structures]].

## Rates (as of Nov 2026)
| Taxable income | Rate | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Up to AED 375,000 | 0% | Decree-Law 47/2022 Art. 3 + Cabinet Decision 116/2022 [1] |
| Above AED 375,000 | 9% | [1] |
| Qualifying income of a Qualifying Free Zone Person | 0% | Art. 18 [1] |
| MNE groups ≥ EUR 750m revenue | Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax (15% per press; not stated on MoF page) from FY starting 1 Jan 2025 | [5] — not relevant to the owner |

## Who is taxable
- **Companies** (mainland and free zone): taxable persons from incorporation.
- **Individuals doing business** (sole establishments, freelancers): taxable only if **combined turnover from all their business activities exceeds AED 1,000,000 in a calendar year** (Cabinet Decision 49/2023, Art. 2) [2]. Wages, personal investment income and real estate investment income are excluded at any amount [2]; property income stays excluded only while no licence is required (a holiday-home permit counts as a licence; Ejari does not) [13].
- Implication: one person running three ventures as sole establishments has **one** AED 1m test across all three.

## Registration
- Companies formed on or after 1 Mar 2024: register within **3 months** of incorporation (FTA Decision 3/2024) [9].
- Individuals: register by **31 March** of the year after the year turnover first exceeded AED 1m [9].
- Late registration: **AED 10,000** [4]. FTA waiver initiative (as of May 2026): automatically waived if the first return is filed within 7 months of the end of the first tax period; already-paid penalties credited to EmaraTax [6]. No end date stated — don't rely on it.

## Small Business Relief (SBR)
- Election available if revenue is **≤ AED 3m** in the current period *and every previous period* (MD 73/2023) [3].
- Now available for tax periods ending **on or before 31 Dec 2029** (MD 131/2026) [3][8]. Applies to 2026.
- **Not available to** Qualifying Free Zone Persons or members of MNE groups.
- Cost of electing: that year's tax losses and net interest cannot be carried forward.
- **Anti-fragmentation:** if a business is artificially split and combined revenue exceeds AED 3m, the FTA can deny relief under the general anti-abuse rule (Art. 50). Separate ventures with genuinely different businesses (golf goods, events, farming) should be distinguishable — judgement; confirm with an adviser.
- Electing still requires registration, a (simplified) return and records [7].

## Free zone companies — Qualifying Free Zone Person (QFZP)
Conditions (Art. 18 [1], Cabinet Decision 100/2023): adequate substance in the free zone; earn qualifying income; no election for the standard rate; transfer-pricing compliance; **audited financial statements** (MD 84/2025) [11]; non-qualifying revenue within the **de minimis** limit — lower of 5% of revenue or AED 5m (per DLA Piper/PwC; Ministerial Decision number not verified). Qualifying activities are now set by **MD 229/2025**, which repealed MD 265/2023 [10]. Losing status reportedly lasts for the period plus the next four (not confirmed in the law text read this run).

Judgement: a QFZP selling to UAE mainland consumers (DCS Golf DTC, Wednesday Circuit corporate clients) will mostly earn non-qualifying income — the free zone 0% rate rarely helps a local consumer business; SBR and the AED 375k band do more.

## Filing, payment, records
- Return **and payment** within **9 months** of the end of the tax period (Art. 53) [1][7].
- Records retained **7 years** (Art. 56) [1].
- Related-party / connected-person disclosure may be required with the return; master/local file only above thresholds (Art. 55) [1]. Payments to the owner (salary, rent, loans) are connected-person transactions — document them at arm's length.
- Audited accounts: required above AED 50m revenue, for tax groups, and for QFZPs (MD 84/2025) [11].
- Tax group: parent must hold ≥ 95% of capital, votes, profits and net assets (Art. 40) [1].
- **E-invoicing** (MD 243 and 244/2025): businesses below AED 50m revenue must appoint an accredited service provider by **31 Mar 2027** and go live **1 Jul 2027**; B2C currently out of scope [12].

## Key penalties (Cabinet Decision 75/2023 as amended, Mar 2024 consolidation) [4]
| Violation | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Late registration | AED 10,000 |
| Failure to keep records | AED 10,000 (AED 20,000 repeat within 24 months) |
| Late return | AED 500/month for 12 months, then AED 1,000/month |
| Late payment | 14% per year, monthly |
| Late deregistration | AED 1,000/month, max AED 10,000 |
No newer Corporate Tax penalty amendment was found; a 2026 VAT/excise penalty reform (Cabinet Decision 129/2025) exists — confirm whether CT penalties changed.

## Worked example (illustrative AED)
Three ventures in their second year:

| Scenario | Revenue | Taxable profit | Tax |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. One LLC per venture, each revenue < 3m, SBR elected | 0.8m / 1.2m / 0.6m | 150k / 300k / –50k | **0** (but the farm's loss can't be carried forward in an SBR year) |
| B. Same, no SBR | same | 150k / 300k / –50k | 0 / 0 / 0 — each under AED 375k anyway; farm keeps its loss |
| C. All three as owner's sole establishments | 2.6m combined (> AED 1m → taxable) | 400k combined | SBR elected → 0; without SBR (400k − 375k) × 9% = **AED 2,250** |
| D. One LLC, profit 900k, revenue 3.5m | 3.5m | 900k | (900k − 375k) × 9% = **AED 47,250**; SBR unavailable |

Lessons: (1) while profits are small, the AED 375k band alone usually brings tax to zero, so **don't elect SBR for a loss-making venture** (Al Ain farm, early years) — keep the loss; (2) separate companies each get their own AED 375k band — but the FTA can challenge splits made only for tax.

## Key takeaways
- 0% to AED 375k, 9% above; SBR (≤ AED 3m revenue) extended to 2029.
- Sole traders: AED 1m combined-turnover trigger across all their businesses.
- Register within 3 months of incorporation; file and pay within 9 months; keep records 7 years.
- Free zone 0% needs audited accounts, substance and qualifying income — rarely useful for local B2C.
- E-invoicing arrives for small businesses on 1 Jul 2027 — choose accounting software accordingly.

## What this means for the empire
- **DCS Golf / Wednesday Circuit:** set up as companies (limited liability matters more than tax at this size); register for CT within 3 months; consider SBR each year only where profitable.
- **Al Ain Ostrich Farm:** greenfield losses are valuable — do **not** elect SBR in loss years; keep audited-quality books from day one.
- **Calendar:** put CT registration, the 9-month return/payment deadline, and the e-invoicing provider deadline (31 Mar 2027) into the app's compliance tracker.
- Keep personal rental property unlicensed and personal to stay outside CT (see [[UAE Holding Structures]]).
- A licensed UAE tax adviser should confirm: SBR anti-fragmentation risk across the three ventures, sole-establishment vs company choice, and any QFZP plans.
