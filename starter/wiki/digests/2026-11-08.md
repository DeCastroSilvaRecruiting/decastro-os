## The one thing that matters
Keep Dubai rental property out of any company that relies on the free zone 0% rate. Held personally and unlicensed, rent is outside Corporate Tax at any amount; inside a free zone holdco it is taxed at 9% and can breach the de minimis test, costing the holdco its 0% status for five years. Structure as: ventures under a light ADGM/DIFC holdco, property held personally (with a DIFC/ADJD will) or in a separate SPV — and have a licensed UAE tax adviser confirm before forming anything.

**Ventures (second run):** all three ventures are gated by *permission* questions before demand — DCS Golf's home licence, the Al Ain farm's right to keep ostriches and source birds, Wednesday Circuit's access to peak tee times. Each can be answered with a few written enquiries costing almost nothing; get those answers before spending on brand, stock or land.
