## Researched today
- [[Amazon FBA from China: Model, Rules and Economics]] (verified and upgraded) — Amazon's own sell.amazon.ae rate card (FBA fees effective 1 Aug 2025) confirms every referral rate, the FBA fee bands, AED 2/cu ft storage and the zero-subscription promotion; Dubai Customs, the FTA and MoF confirm 5% duty on CIF, the AED 375k/187.5k VAT thresholds, 9% CT and Small Business Relief to 2029. Corrected: VAT-registered importers settle import VAT in the return, not at the border; Amazon fees carry 5% VAT; worked-example FBA fee is AED 9.50 (net AED 34.55/unit, or ~AED 32.45 with post-Hormuz freight). Seller-eligibility rules (trade licence, policy text, Brand Registry offices) remain unverified behind the Seller Central login. Confidence stays medium.
- [[UAE E-commerce Seller Set-up: Licence, VAT and Corporate Tax]] (new) — consumer sales are an excluded activity for the free zone 0% rate, so a B2C Amazon.ae store gains nothing from a free zone and adds mainland-access friction (DET permit ~AED 10,000/yr or a dual licence). Year-one recommendation (judgement): Dubai mainland DET LLC with an online-retail activity, own Dubai Customs code (AED 120), CT registration within 3 months. CT is nil at AED 400–600k revenue under any structure.
- [[China Sourcing Playbook for UAE Private-Label Importers]] (new) — buy via Alibaba Trade Assurance or verified Global Sources suppliers, check licences on gsxt.gov.cn, NNN agreement and China trademark before sharing designs, pay 30/70 with the balance released only after a passed inspection (USD 268–298 per man-day, named providers), buy FOB/FCA and clear under your own code. The Strait of Hormuz has been effectively closed to container shipping since 28 Feb 2026 (still so in early Nov): China–UAE cargo now lands at Khor Fakkan, Fujairah or Sohar and is trucked in, at roughly 2–4x pre-war sea rates and 6–8 weeks door-to-FBA.

## The one thing that matters
For the Global Trend Store, VAT and freight — not Corporate Tax — are the live numbers. Imports count towards the AED 375,000 VAT test, so a seller on ~AED 400k of sales and ~AED 140k of stock imports must register within 30 days of crossing it (AED 10,000 penalty if late); and the Hormuz closure has pushed sea freight to about AED 5 a unit and lead time to ~4.5 months to first sale, so the Commerce Lab's AED 3 freight default and two-month lead time are now too optimistic. Choose the entity (mainland DET LLC) on liability, Amazon/bank acceptance and running cost, and have a licensed UAE tax adviser confirm the natural-persons exclusion and VAT timing before forming it.
